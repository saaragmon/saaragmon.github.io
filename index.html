Optimization

JIT Single-Machine Scheduling

A hybrid Genetic Algorithm + Linear Programming system for Just-In-Time single-machine scheduling, minimizing weighted earliness and tardiness penalties. Combines order crossover, ranking selection, swap mutation, and elitism with PuLP-based LP result caching. Includes multi-sheet Excel workbooks with Solver models, Gantt charts, and penalty analysis, plus a full defense reference covering the LP formulation and approximation proofs.
